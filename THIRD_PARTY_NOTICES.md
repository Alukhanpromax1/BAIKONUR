## Assets

There are no third-party images, 3D models, textures, audio recordings or video in this repository.
Planets, stars and the spacecraft are generated procedurally in code; the favicon, brand mark,
character portraits and diagrams are original vector drawings; all sound is synthesised at runtime.

NASA's 3D Resources were considered and not used. No NASA logo, insignia, flag or mission identifier
is used as branding.
